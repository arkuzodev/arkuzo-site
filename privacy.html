Privacy Policy

How Arkuzo treats data, visitors, and privacy.

1. Overview of Data Protection

Arkuzo is designed as a privacy-friendly static showcase and community hub. We do not operate tracking databases, we do not profile users, and we do not sell or monetize personal data.

Interactive features on this website (such as the Choose Your Path selection and Project Brief builder) operate entirely inside your local browser storage. No form data, selection state, or project brief content is sent to our servers or third-party tracking services.

3. Data Collection on Our Website

Server Log Files:

When you access our static website, technical data required for secure and stable delivery is automatically recorded by the web host (such as browser type, operating system, referrer URL, host name of the accessing computer, and time of server request). This data is processed based on legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR to ensure technical stability and prevent abuse.

Cookies & Local Storage:

We do not use tracking cookies or marketing cookies. We use browser Local Storage solely for user preferences (for example, keeping your chosen role selected on the Choose Your Path module). The Project Brief Builder runs 100% locally in your browser and does not transmit any inputs or drafts over the network.

4. External Services & Links

Discord:

Our website links to our official Discord community at https://discord.gg/arkuzo. When you click an external Discord link, you leave our website and connect directly to Discord's servers. Discord processes data under its own terms and privacy policy (https://discord.com/privacy).

5. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to receive free information about the origin, recipient, and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have the right to request the correction, restriction, or deletion of this data where applicable under data protection laws.

If you have any questions regarding your data rights, you may contact us at any time using the contact details in the Impressum.
